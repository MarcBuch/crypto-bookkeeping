Agentic LP Market Making

A practical architecture for combining deterministic trading tools with planner, reviewer, execution, verification, and performance-feedback agents.

Preservation first Fees second Human approval initially

Current foundation

Read and measure

Position lifecycle, P&L, fees, IL, history, snapshots, and hedge reconstruction already exist.

Main gap

Closed-loop decisions

Proposals, approvals, executions, reconciliations, and outcomes are not yet one persistent workflow.

Tool boundary

Deterministic math

If identical inputs should produce identical outputs, implement it as a tested tool.

Agent boundary

Ambiguous synthesis

Keep conflicting-signal interpretation, narrative, and approval requests agentic.

Operating principle

Tools calculate. Agents decide. Risk controls can veto.

The agent must not recalculate financial facts from prose or invent thresholds. It consumes a canonical, timestamped market snapshot and produces a structured intent that deterministic policy code can challenge.

flowchart LR A[Market adapters] --> B[Canonical market snapshot] B --> C[Calculators and simulations] C --> D[Policy and risk checks] D --> E[Planner agent] E --> F[Human approval] F --> G[Execution adapter] G --> H[Verification and reconciliation] H --> I[Performance attribution] I --> B

The agentic workflow

This is the trading equivalent of plan, build, test, deploy, observe. Each stage has a narrow responsibility and produces an auditable artifact.

sequenceDiagram participant M as Market data participant T as Deterministic tools participant P as Planner agent participant R as Risk reviewer participant H as Human participant X as Execution adapter participant V as Verification participant L as Ledger M->>T: Fetch and normalize snapshot T->>P: Position, regime, P&L, hedge, scenarios P->>R: Structured trade intent R-->>P: Pass, warnings, or veto P->>H: Explain proposal and residual risks H->>X: Approve exact intent X->>V: Simulate and submit bounded action V->>L: Receipts, fills, balances, new position L->>T: Persist outcome and refresh snapshot T->>P: Expected versus realized performance

1. Observe

Create one immutable snapshot containing position range and inventory, fees, capital appreciation, divergence loss, hedge state, signed drift/vol, funding, pool economics, borrow health, and data freshness.

2. Plan

The planner chooses HOLD, RERANGE, HEDGE, REDUCE, or CLOSE and emits an exact intent, rationale, confidence, expected horizon, and abort conditions.

3. Review

An independent risk module checks hard limits. It can veto the planner. A second agent may explain the challenge, but numeric vetoes belong to code.

4. Execute and verify

Execution revalidates state, simulates, enforces slippage and deadlines, submits only the approved action, then reconciles receipts, fills, balances, range, and hedge.

Strategy encoded as policy

The policy is not a generic yield optimizer. It follows PLAYBOOK.md: protect USDC repayment capital first, then earn fees. HYPE upside is explicitly out of scope for this book.

flowchart TD A{Position state} -->|Upper third| B[Fee continuity only] A -->|Middle third| C[Hold] A -->|Lower third| D[Defend] E{Signed drift/vol} -->|Above +0.5| F[Keep earning] E -->|-0.5 to +0.5| G[Wide range, normal discipline] E -->|-1.0 to -0.5| H[Reduce, widen, skew, hedge] E -->|Below -1.0| I[Reduce hard, close, repay, or bridge hedge] D --> J[Widen, reduce, hedge, close, or repay]

Hard invariants

  • Never hedge more than 100% of live LP WHYPE delta; Mode A targets 50–75%.
  • Do not hedge a mostly-USDC position near the upper bound; that creates naked short exposure.
  • Do not tight-recenter into lower-third weakness. Reduce, widen, repay, or close first.
  • Maintain Aave health factor and a USDC repayment reserve above configured floors.
  • Reject stale data, excessive slippage, unbounded size, and fee economics that cannot recover churn.

Structured intent, not prose

The planner should emit a machine-checkable object. Narrative is generated alongside it, never used as the execution contract.

{
  "action": "RERANGE",
  "range": { "lower": 52, "upper": 78 },
  "capitalPct": 75,
  "hedgeDeltaPct": 50,
  "expectedHoldingDays": 10,
  "abortConditions": [
    "slippage > 0.30%",
    "healthFactor < 1.8",
    "snapshotAge > 5 minutes"
  ],
  "confidence": 0.72
}

What remains agentic

Synthesis

Choose between valid actions when regime, fee, hedge, and news signals conflict.

Context

Assess whether a catalyst is durable and compare conditions with previous reviews.

Communication

Explain the trade-off, ask for approval, and write the decision journal.

Performance feedback

Judge the strategy by attribution, not by whether the token rose. Every decision should be evaluated against counterfactuals.

flowchart LR A[Executed decision] --> B[Immediate execution quality] B --> C[Daily fees, carry, delta, risk] C --> D[Lifecycle P&L attribution] D --> E[Monthly policy evaluation] E --> F[Update thresholds, prompts, and tests] F --> A

Net P&L = LP capital appreciation + fee income + hedge price P&L + funding − transaction costs − financing costs

Compare against holding the original inventory, LP without hedging, LP without reranging, and the rejected alternative.

Rollout recommendation

01

Shadow mode

Generate proposals and score them without trading.

02

Human approval

Execute only an explicitly approved, hashed intent.

03

Bounded automation

Automate low-risk actions within strict limits.

04

Emergency controls

Permit only predefined safety reductions or closures.

Summary

The safest architecture is a deep deterministic tool layer wrapped by a deliberately constrained agentic workflow. Codify facts, calculations, simulations, thresholds, and reconciliation. Keep judgment, explanation, and approval agentic. Start with observation and shadow decisions; add execution last.

Grounded in PLAYBOOK.md and the current core, CLI, API, and hedge/P&L architecture. Proposed execution modules are explicitly future work.
